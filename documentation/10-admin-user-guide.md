# 10 — Admin User Guide

## 1. Accessing the Admin Panel
1. Navigate to `/admin/login` on the website.
2. Enter your admin email and password.
3. Click **Login**.

> **Note:** Admin accounts are created by a system administrator. Contact your project manager if you need access.

## 2. Dashboard
After logging in, you will see the **Dashboard** with summary metrics:
- Total inquiries (unread count highlighted)
- Total consultation requests
- Total training registrations
- Recent submissions

## 3. Managing Content

### 3.1 Services
- **View all services** — see the full list with title, category, and order.
- **Add a service** — fill in title, slug, summary, category, image, scope items, and display order.
- **Edit a service** — modify any field and save.
- **Delete a service** — remove with confirmation prompt.

### 3.2 Projects
- **View all projects** — filterable by category and year.
- **Add a project** — provide title, slug, client, location, year, summary, scope, services, and image.
- **Edit/Delete** — same pattern as services.

### 3.3 Courses
- **View all courses** — filterable by category.
- **Add a course** — title, slug, category, duration, duration unit, certification body, summary, objectives, audience, and curriculum.
- **Edit/Delete** — same pattern as services.

> **Important:** Do not publish course fees, schedules, or instructor names without written CONSOL approval.

### 3.4 News
- **View all articles** — sorted by date, filterable by category.
- **Add an article** — title, slug, excerpt, date, category, body paragraphs, and image.
- **Edit/Delete** — same pattern as services.

### 3.5 Events
- **View all events** — sorted by date.
- **Add an event** — title, slug, date, location, description, and image.
- **Edit/Delete** — same pattern as services.

## 4. Managing Submissions

### 4.1 Inquiries
- View all contact form submissions.
- Filter by read/unread status.
- Click an inquiry to view full details (name, email, phone, subject, message).
- Mark as read/unread.

### 4.2 Consultations
- View all consultation requests.
- See requester details, selected service, preferred contact method.
- Mark as read/unread.

### 4.3 Training Registrations
- View all training registration submissions.
- See registrant details and the course they registered for.
- Mark as read/unread.

## 5. Your Account
- **View profile** — click your name in the top-right corner.
- **Logout** — click the Logout button.

## 6. Best Practices
1. Review and respond to inquiries within 24 hours.
2. Keep project portfolio up to date with completed engagements.
3. Publish news regularly to keep the website current.
4. Never publish unverified information or unapproved client details.
5. Always log out when finished, especially on shared computers.
